KerBridge · NAS Access

This address signs you in to your shared drives

It is used by the NAS Access app on your computer, not by you. There is nothing to do on this page.

Opening a shared drive or network folder
Open it the way you always do. NAS Access signs you in in the background, with your work account.
If you are asked for a username and password
Open NAS Access from the system tray, next to the clock, and sign in there. Then try the drive again.
If it still does not work
Ask your IT support, and tell them which drive you were opening and what the message said. Sending them a screenshot of NAS Access helps.
KerBridge lets your computer reach the file server with your ordinary work account, so there is no separate password to remember.
